Your link:

https://youtu.be/z-H8vkIFptc?si=V4M5PTDHWTlpUV3K


This is a share link, not an embed link.

👉 <iframe> cannot play normal or shortened YouTube URLs.

✅ CORRECT WAY to USE YouTube in <iframe>
🔹 Step 1: Extract the video ID

From your link:

z-H8vkIFptc (after / and before ?)

🔹 Step 2: Convert to EMBED URL format

✅ Correct embed format

https://www.youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID


So your final link becomes:

https://www.youtube.com/embed/z-H8vkIFptc

✅ WORKING <iframe> CODE (USE THIS)
<iframe
    width="600"
    height="350"
    src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/z-H8vkIFptc"
    title="YouTube video"
    frameborder="0"
    allowfullscreen>
</iframe>


✔️ This WILL work in all browsers.


🧠 ONE-LINE VIVA ANSWER (VERY IMPORTANT)

YouTube videos must be embedded using the /embed/VIDEO_ID URL format, not the normal or shortened video link.
